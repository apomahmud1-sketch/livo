You must be 18 years or older. By using Livo you confirm you are 18+.

## 2. Coins and billing

- Coin purchases are final, except where the law or the store requires a refund.

Hosts earn diamonds from calls and gifts, minus the platform commission
shown in the app. Withdrawals need admin approval and may take time to process.

people's private information. We may suspend or ban accounts that break
these rules.

## 5. Availability
We may change, suspend or stop features at any time, including for
maintenance. Prices, call rates and coin packs can be changed by the
admin; the prices shown in the app at the time of a call apply.
