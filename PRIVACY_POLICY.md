Livo is a premium 1-to-1 video calling app for adults (18+).

## Data we collect
- Account data you provide: name, profile photo, country, language, age.
- Call data: call duration, billed minutes, gifts sent/received (for billing
  and host earnings). We do not record the audio or video of your calls.
- Payment data: recharge and withdrawal records (processed by the payment
  provider; we do not store full card or bank details).

## How we use it
To run the app, bill calls per minute, pay hosts, prevent fraud, and
answer support requests.

## Data sharing
We do not sell your personal data. Data is shared only with the service
providers needed to run the app (video calling, hosting, payments).

## Your rights
You can request a copy or deletion of your data, and delete your account
from Settings. Contact us at the support email above.

## Age
Livo is for users aged 18 and over only.
